# Universal Rule: Padding & Spacing

## Mandatory Padding
- **ALWAYS ADD GENEROUS PADDING**: Every card, picture frame, container, banner, and content section MUST have ample top, bottom, and side padding between its borders/edges and its internal text or elements.
- Never let text, labels, numbers, or interactive elements sit close to or hug card/frame borders.
- Default to spacious internal padding (e.g. `px-8 py-8 sm:px-10 sm:py-9 md:px-12 md:py-10` or greater on cards, and generous `paddingTop`/`paddingBottom` on image/media frames).
