# Universal Project Rules

## Padding & Spacing (STRICT & UNIVERSAL)
- **ALWAYS ADD GENEROUS PADDING**: Every card, picture frame, container, banner, and content section MUST have ample top, bottom, and side padding between its borders/edges and its internal text or elements.
- Never let text, labels, numbers, or interactive elements sit close to or touch card/frame borders.
- Default to generous spacing: e.g. for stat cards use `px-8 py-8 sm:px-10 sm:py-9 md:px-12 md:py-10` or greater, ensuring clean visual breathing room.
